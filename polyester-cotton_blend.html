Polyester-Cotton (Polycotton)

Polyester-cotton, often called polycotton, is a blended fabric that combines the strength and wrinkle resistance of Polyester with the softness and breathability of Cotton.

Overview

Polycotton blends are engineered to balance comfort and durability. By mixing synthetic and natural fibers, manufacturers create fabrics that are easier to care for, more resistant to shrinking, and longer lasting than 100% cotton garments.

Common blend ratios include 65% polyester / 35% cotton and 50% polyester / 50% cotton, though variations exist depending on intended use.

Key Properties

Wrinkle Resistant

Polyester reduces creasing compared to pure cotton fabrics.

Breathable

Cotton content maintains airflow and comfort.

Durable

Stronger and more tear-resistant than 100% cotton.

Easy Care

Often machine washable with minimal ironing required.

Common Uses

  • T-shirts and casual wear
  • School and work uniforms
  • Bed sheets and pillowcases
  • Table linens
  • Hospital and hospitality textiles

Care Instructions

  • Machine wash warm or cold.
  • Tumble dry low.
  • Iron on medium heat if needed.
  • Avoid excessive heat to protect polyester fibers.

Environmental Considerations

Polycotton combines biodegradable natural fiber (Cotton) with synthetic polyester, which is petroleum-based and not biodegradable. Blended fabrics can be more difficult to recycle due to mixed fiber content.
