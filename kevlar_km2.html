Kevlar KM2
Kevlar KM2 is a specialized high-performance aramid fiber variant designed for advanced ballistic protection.

Kevlar KM2 is a variant of the renowned Kevlar fiber developed for military and law enforcement applications. It offers superior impact resistance, flexibility, and lightweight performance, making it ideal for body armor and tactical gear.

Kevlar KM2 retains the inherent flame resistance and high tensile strength characteristic of Kevlar, but has optimized properties for enhanced ballistic performance.

History and Development

Kevlar KM2 was developed in response to the need for lightweight, high-performance body armor for soldiers and law enforcement. It builds upon the original Kevlar technology to deliver improved resistance against bullets and fragmentation.

Key Properties

Property Description
Tensile Strength Extremely high; similar to standard Kevlar but optimized for ballistic applications
Lightweight Reduces fatigue for wearers of protective gear
Ballistic Resistance Enhanced protection against bullets and shrapnel
Flame Resistance Maintains inherent heat and flame resistance
Flexibility Improved drape and comfort for wearable armor

Applications

  • Military body armor and tactical vests
  • Law enforcement protective gear
  • Ballistic helmets and shields
  • Cut- and puncture-resistant gloves
  • Aerospace and vehicle armor composites

Care and Handling

  • Store in a cool, dry environment away from UV light
  • Inspect regularly for wear or fiber damage
  • Follow manufacturer guidance for cleaning tactical or protective clothing
  • Avoid prolonged exposure to chemicals that may degrade fibers
